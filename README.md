🌾 VILCON – Village Connect

Connecting Villages. Empowering Communities.

VILCON (Village Connect) is a technology-driven rural connectivity platform designed to bridge the digital divide between rural and urban communities. It provides villagers with a centralized digital platform to access essential services, healthcare information, educational resources, government schemes, agricultural support, and community updates.

The platform focuses on improving accessibility, strengthening rural communication, and making essential information available even in areas with limited internet connectivity.
